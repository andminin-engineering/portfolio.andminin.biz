Cupones No Ganadores: certified RNG draws

Technical leadCertified RNGLotería Correntina

End-to-end system behind Lotería Correntina's second-chance draws among non-winning Quiniela coupons: batch extraction from the central system, a certified RNG engine consumed as a REST service, per-zone draws with a verifiable evidence package, a broadcast presenter for TV, and public publication.

Draw lifecycle

  • Extraction. Non-winning coupons are consolidated from the central system on a configurable schedule.
  • Draw. Draws run per period and per zone, closing with a downloadable package of verifiable evidence.
  • Publication. A presenter application drives the TV broadcast; results stay on the public site for 15 days.

Assurance

  • Designed the certified RNG engine, a Spring Boot REST service.
  • Presented in ALEA audits and against WLA-SCS:2016 (built on ISO/IEC 27001), which requires independent RNG verification before an electronic draw can go live.
  • Prepared the services, black-box tests, and audit reports.
  • Draws are audited by a public notary.

Operations and stack

  • Administration panel with an annual calendar, draws by calendar and zone, and role management.
  • Spring Boot services; Next.js for administration and the public results site.

Public evidence

  • Public winners site
  • Broadcast draw, June 2024

From manual releases to continuous delivery

Software delivery leadAWSGitHub Actions

Delivery practice for the provider's production applications, from environment strategy to release gates and cloud governance.

Continuous delivery

  • Led the migration of more than five production applications from manual deployment on nginx servers to continuous deployment on AWS with GitHub Actions.
  • Releases went from 15–30 minutes at best, and hours with fixes or rollbacks, to minutes.
  • Automated tests and telemetry gates became conditions for advancing through the pipeline.

Environments

  • Replaced a two-environment setup (functional testing and production) with per-branch environments for dev, QA, and higher stages.
  • Brought deployment practice from infrastructure into development (shell scripting; nginx, Tomcat, AWS) while keeping ownership of production deployments.

Cloud governance and coordination

  • Established governance standards for AWS accounts and environments — access, segmentation, and deployment conventions — across product lines onboarded onto the platform.
  • Coordinated development, QA, infrastructure, business, and external vendors: release planning, acceptance criteria, architecture review, and risk management.

Web app · Small merchants · WhatsApp API

ValeFiado

A digital credit notebook for small merchants: record what a customer takes on credit and what they pay, so both sides see the same account. It records credit only and does not move money.

The merchant creates an order and sends the customer an acceptance link through the WhatsApp API. Accepting it also establishes the merchant–customer relationship, and both parties' acceptance shows in the account movements. Orders can also be accepted by scanning a QR code; if that fails, in-app messaging shows the order to registered customers as pending acceptance.
